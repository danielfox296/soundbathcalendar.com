The calendar has 135 sound bath sessions on the books across the Front Range as this goes up, running through October 19. Ninety-two are in the Denver area, seventeen in and around Boulder, fourteen in and around Fort Collins, and twelve in Colorado Springs. These are the venues with the most of them.

## 1545 South Pearl Street · Denver

Seventeen sessions in three weeks at one address in Platt Park, and no other venue on the calendar has more than six. The building appears as two listings: [the address itself](/venue/1545-s-pearl-st/), where Soul Growth Network runs eleven, and [Green Light Reiki](/venue/green-light-reiki/), which carries six. August's roundup counted fourteen here.

Soul Growth Network runs a [Saturday Morning Sound Bath](/event/saturday-morning-sound-bath-2026-10-03-1545-s-pearl-st/) and a [Sunday Morning Sound Bath](/event/sunday-morning-sound-bath-2026-10-04-1545-s-pearl-st/) at 10am on the weekends of October 3, 10 and 17. Both cost $33 on the first two weekends, and the October 17 and 18 listings don't give a price. On Monday evenings at 6:45pm, from September 28 through October 19, it holds the [Alchemy Series](/event/alchemy-series7-stages-of-personal-transformationguided-sound-meditation-2026-10-05-1545-s-pearl-st/), a seven-part guided meditation with gongs, singing bowls, chimes and drums, at $44 for the first three dates. Its one afternoon session is Vinny's [Sacred Healing Mantras & Spiritual Conversation](/event/sacred-healing-mantras-spiritual-conversation-universal-sounds-and-vibra-2026-10-03-1545-s-pearl-st/), Saturday, October 3 at 4pm, also $33.

The building's next session after the September 28 Alchemy date is Green Light Reiki's [Yoga and Sound Bath](/event/yoga-and-sound-bath-2026-09-29-green-light-reiki/) with Yelena Sergeyevna, Tuesday, September 29 at 7pm for $33.85. She teaches it again on October 6 at the same price and on October 13, which has no price listed. Green Light Reiki also has a 45-minute [Relax & Unwind Sound Bath](/event/relax-unwind-sound-bath-2026-09-30-green-light-reiki/) on singing bowls and chimes on Wednesday, September 30 at 6pm for $30, Nikki Dorn's [Moon Tribe Women's Witch Circle](/event/moon-tribe-womens-witch-circle-2026-10-01-green-light-reiki/) on Thursday, October 1 at 7pm for $23.18, and an [Energy Healing + Sound Bath Immersion](/event/energy-healing-sound-bath-immersion-2026-10-04-green-light-reiki/) on Sunday, October 4 at 4pm for $44.52.

## Grow + Gather · Englewood

Six sessions in the rooftop greenhouse at 900 East Hampden Avenue, all of them in the morning. Finding Center Peace runs [Morning Yoga + Sound in the Greenhouse](/event/morning-yoga-sound-in-the-greenhouse-at-grow-gather-2026-09-30-grow-gather/) on Wednesdays and Fridays at 9am, starting Wednesday, September 30 and running through October 9. It's an hour of yoga that ends with about fifteen minutes of sound meditation, for $15. Angela of Kali-Prana Yoga leads a 75-minute [greenhouse yoga flow](/event/growgather-greenhouse-yoga-soundflow-2026-10-03-grow-gather/) at 10am on Saturday, October 3 and Saturday, October 10, closing with a sound savasana played by a rotating sound guide. Neither Saturday lists a price.

Five sessions at [the venue on Soderburg Drive](/venue/rocky-mountain-restore-stretch/), and every one is a gong journey. ## How this list was made

The only measure is how many upcoming sessions each place had on the calendar on September 28, 2026, when this was written. Dates and prices come from the listings' ticket data, and prices are printed to the cent. The Platt Park count is by street address; every other count is by venue as listed. "Next" means the first session after September 28, and the seven venues with four sessions each appear in order of their next one. Of the 135 sessions, 94 publish a single price (nine of them free), 20 publish a range, and 21 publish no price, and the median among the 85 paid single prices is $33.85. Listings change often, so [the calendar](/) has the current list, and [the map](/map/) shows where every venue is.
