Venues worth knowing right now

The places keeping the most sessions on the calendar this month, what's on at each, what it costs, and what's next. One address in Platt Park is running fourteen of them.

Roundup · By Sound Bath Calendar · August 10, 2026

The calendar tracks every sound bath we can find across the Front Range: 124 sessions on the books as this goes up, running through August 31. Seventy-two are in Denver, twenty in Colorado Springs, seventeen in Fort Collins, and fifteen in Boulder. This is a plain cut of that list: the places with the most on it right now, what they charge, and what's next at each.

One measure only, the count. No ranking beyond it, and no reviews.

1545 South Pearl Street · Denver

Fourteen sessions this month at a single address, which makes a small shared wellness space on the South Pearl Street block in Platt Park the busiest building on the calendar by a wide margin. No venue page shows that, because four practitioners at that address list under their own names: the address itself carries six, Green Light Reiki carries six across two spellings, and Soul Growth Network carries two.

The programming rotates through chakra work, reiki, breathwork, pranayama and a plain Saturday morning bath. Green Light Reiki publishes $30 for most of its sessions, which is the low end for an indoor Denver evening, and Inner Alignment on Wednesday, August 19 is listed at $60. The rest send you to the ticket page. Next up at the address: Himalayan Kriya, Breathwork and Sound Journey on Wednesday, August 12, at $30.

Sacred Society · Denver

Eight sessions, more than any other single venue, at a wellness center and coffee cafe under one roof in the Highlands. It also runs the widest published price band on the calendar: $30 for the candlelight yoga nidra on August 20, $45 for Stretch and Sound on the 27th and the Sacred Rest Ritual on the 30th, $60 for the new moon and full moon baths, $65 for the restorative one. A somatic sound workshop on Saturday the 22nd runs longer than the rest. Next: New Moon Sound Bath on Thursday, August 13, at $60.

Rocky Mountain Restore & Stretch · Fort Collins

Six sessions, and the steadiest schedule of the month. The venue is a sound-healing space called The Golden House, west of Fort Collins near Horsetooth Reservoir, built around a five-foot gong named Iris. The spine is a weekly Thursday gong journey, with a Sunday evening version on the 23rd and an outdoor eclipse session on the 12th. Next: Solar Eclipse New Moon Outdoor Foothills Sound Bath on Wednesday, August 12, outdoors, $47.

Singing Bowls of the Rockies · Colorado Springs

Four sessions at the studio on South Sierra Madre Street downtown, all at $39, which is the most consistent price on the calendar. The themes change every time: an eclipse session, a new moon session on breath and connection, a Saturday on letting go, a Sunday afternoon. The same organizer also has one of the cave sessions below. Next: Into the Shadow: Solar Eclipse Sound Bath on Wednesday, August 12.

Cave of the Winds Mountain Park · Colorado Springs

The most unusual space on the calendar, and the only one where the session is held underground. Three different organizers have sessions in the cave this month. Temple of Echoes on Sunday, August 16 is $66, the top of the published range this month. A second session follows on Friday the 21st, and a run of full moon sessions closes out the month at $65. Check the ticket page for what the walk in involves before you book.

Harmonist Sanctuary · Denver

The yoga and event space on East Colfax reads differently from the others, because the space is rented out and its calendar is a mix rather than a program. Sound Murals has a gong journey on Wednesday the 12th and another on the 26th. Liminal Lemonade has a monthly candlelight bath on the 19th at $33. Next: the gong journey on Wednesday, August 12.

The eclipse sessions

Eight sessions are timed to an eclipse this month, which is the largest cluster around any single occasion on the calendar. Seven of them land on August 11 and 12, across all four metros. In Boulder on Tuesday the 11th: an Eclipse Sound Journey and a Solar Eclipse Sunset Gong Bath in North Boulder Park. On Wednesday the 12th: a New Moon in Leo gathering at The Denver Village in Aurora at $44, the outdoor foothills session near Fort Collins at $47, Into the Shadow in Colorado Springs at $39, a dome session at The Seedlings in Fort Collins, and TRANSFORM at The Living Room in Fort Collins at $37.

The eighth is at the end of the month: a Lunar Eclipse Meditation and Six Gong Sound Bath at The StarHouse in the Boulder foothills on Friday, August 28.

If cost is the barrier

Twelve sessions this month are listed free, and fourteen more are donation-based or sliding scale. Among the free ones: sound healing at Green Gables Park in Lakewood and a park session in Boulder, both on Wednesday, August 12; Friday Night Frequencies at Una Vida in Niwot on the 14th; a library session at Smiley Branch in Denver on the 19th; and two on Sunday the 30th, a community session at The Next Us in Colorado Springs and a Sturgeon Moon session at The Denver Village in Aurora. Start there.

How this list was made

One measure only: how many upcoming sessions each place had on the calendar on August 10, 2026, when this was written. Dates, prices and titles come straight from the listings. The Platt Park count is by street address rather than by listing name, because four practitioners at that one space list separately; every other count is by venue as listed. Thirty-nine of the 124 sessions publish a ticket price, and the median among them is $44. Almost all of the published prices sit between $20 and $66, with one day-long retreat at $297 well above the rest. Listings change often, so the calendar is always the current version of the truth, and the map shows where every venue is.
