Practitioner

Beth Naumann

Gong bath

Beth Naumann is an artist in the fullest sense — her practice spans sculpture, end-of-life care and exploration, and sound-based wellness with the gong. At Una Vida Meditation & Movement in Niwot she leads Deep-relaxation Gong Sessions where participants lie down or sit and simply receive, played on a Paiste Synodic Moon Gong — an instrument tuned, as the studio tells it, to the frequency of the moon's travel around Earth.
