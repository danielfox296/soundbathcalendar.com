Zach Miller

Plays The Seedlings Dome

Gong bath

Zach Miller's site puts it simply: sound has "been my way of connecting with the Divine for as long as I can remember." Through Sound Wave Sacrament, Miller serves Fort Collins and Northern Colorado as a certified sound healing practitioner — crystal singing bowls, Tibetan metal bowls, gongs, chimes, and percussion — describing the work as holding space for people who want to bring healing, growth, and spiritual transcendence into their lives, and the practitioner as "a student constantly in a state of learning."
