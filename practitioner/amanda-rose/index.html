Amanda Rose

Plays 929 29th St

Amanda Rose keeps Revive Wellness deliberately open-hearted: a Denver sound healing studio at 929 29th Street whose community sound baths are donation-based and timed to the full and new moons, alongside trauma-informed private sessions and corporate events. An intuitive guide facilitating what they call "heart lead healing spaces," they play crystal bowls, Tibetan bowls, drum, and turtle rattle — and Revive's stated mission says it all: inclusive spaces where people can rest, reconnect, and remember their own capacity to heal.
