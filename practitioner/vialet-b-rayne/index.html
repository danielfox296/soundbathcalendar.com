Vialet B Rayne

Vialet B Rayne founded Discover Your Spiritual Gifts in Centennial and filled it with sound: she organizes and plays in the center's Sacred Sound Baths and the five-practitioner Sound Immersion with the Harmonic Quintessence, practicing Divine Sound Healing alongside Angelic Reiki (Master Teacher), Akashic Records reading, and spiritual mentorship. The road there ran through a Master's in Human Resource Development, a decade-plus as a college professor, more than 60 self-published books, and founding the Sacred Temple Mystery School in 2017. "Sacredness is the dedication and devotion of holding divine space," she writes — and she built a whole center for it.
