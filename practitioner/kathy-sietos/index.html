Kathy Sietos

Kathy Sietos grew up with the mystical traditions and herbal medicines her Greek immigrant parents carried to the United States, and more than twelve years ago she planted her own tradition on South Pearl Street: Green Light Reiki. A Reiki Master Teacher, Certified Sound Therapist, Yoga Instructor, Meditation Facilitator, and Licensed Massage Therapist, she leads sound immersions that draw on gongs, bowls, drums, and other instruments — a dozen years of Denver healing practice in every session.
