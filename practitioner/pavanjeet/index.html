Pavanjeet

Plays North Boulder Park

Gong bath

Pavanjeet's biography says their teaching "balances the profound with light-hearted play," and the résumé backs both halves: Kundalini Research Institute certification (Levels I and II), Master Gong Practitioner Training with additional Gong Therapy certification, teenage years already deep in yoga and astrology, and seasons lived in Indian ashrams. Their six-gong sound baths land at The StarHouse and North Boulder Park — and online — carried by a practice that also holds Kundalini Bodywork and Core Synchronism certifications. Boulder suits them.
