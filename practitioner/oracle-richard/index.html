Oracle Richard

The Colorado Psychedelic Church's clergy page calls Oracle Richard "a bridge between the seen and unseen," and notes something rarer: he does not offer answers — he helps people ask the right questions. A clergy member working with natural medicines and metaphysical insight, with a stated commitment to accessibility "especially for those harmed by traditional systems," he leads the church's Communal Quartz Sound Bath in Colorado Springs on first Mondays: an hour of being carried by the hum of quartz, then a quiet reintegration with optional sharing.
