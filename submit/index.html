Add to the calendar

This calendar is built by hand from what we can find, which means we miss things and get things wrong. If you run sessions on the Front Range, you are already welcome here. Nothing on this page costs anything.

You run sessions

Send us the next one and we will list it. Recurring sessions we list as they come up, so you only have to tell us once. A listing links out to wherever you actually sell tickets — we are not in the middle of that.

You are already listed

Then you have a profile page we wrote without asking you, which is a strange way to meet. Tell us what is wrong with it, send a photo you would rather we used, or ask us to take it down — we will, no questions.

You have a space

A gallery, a sanctuary, a shop, a studio that sits empty on a Tuesday. Sound baths need very little: floor, quiet, an hour. Tell us about the space and we will put you in touch with practitioners looking for somewhere to play. Also free, and we take nothing from the booking.

Tell us what you have.

What happens next

A person reads every one of these — usually within a few days. We do not list everything we are sent: the calendar covers facilitated group sound sessions on the Front Range, so a yoga class with a bowl at the end is probably not a fit. If yours is not, we will say so rather than leave you wondering.

We use your email to answer you, and to check details if something changes. It does not go on the digest list unless you ask, and it never goes to anyone else. See the privacy page.
