Common questions

What happens at a breathwork and sound session?

You are guided through an active breathing pattern while gongs, bowls, or drums play, then rest into the sound afterward. It is more physically engaging than a lie-still sound bath; the breathwork does real work before the tones settle you.

Is breathwork safe for everyone?

Strong breathwork can bring on lightheadedness, tingling, or strong emotion, and some patterns are not advised during pregnancy or with certain heart, blood-pressure, or seizure conditions. Check with the organizer, and your doctor if unsure, before booking. This is not medical advice.

How much do these sessions cost?

The sessions listed here run free to $65. Some are offered by donation or free. Each listing shows its own price, and the ticket link goes straight to the organizer.
